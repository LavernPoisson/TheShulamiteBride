KEEP YOUR FRIENDS CLOSE

"keep your enemies closer."

This is a saying used loosely in this world. Today I would like to expand on the many meanings of this word, "enemies."

Many people have adopted this saying, without realizing the repercussions and impact it has on life. Based on what we confess and the words we speak, these play a major role in the quality of life.

You see, something must change; it starts with the mindset. When the mind is conditioned to believe that the people in our lives are enemies, the enemy will strategically drive you away from everyone because you will believe that your friends are enemies.

One of the strategies used to get you to fall from grace is isolation, so by making you believe that people are the enemy, it will make you eventually distance yourself and keep you away from others.

Now I'm not saying that people won't hurt you or use you... believe me, there are some people in this world that have chosen to work in the kingdom of darkness. They have chosen to bring destruction to others, including loved ones. That is a choice they made, being fully aware of what they are doing.... These are not the kind of people you should be yoked with. The word of God teaches us not to be yoked with unbelievers. 2 Corinthians 6:14 In this situation it is important to have proper insight and discernment from God. It is very important to know how to deal with this person.

The group of people that I am talking about here are those in our lives that are ignorant to the enemies' devices; they don't even know when they are being used by the devil; they are blinded from the truth of God. They are not working with the enemy to tear you down; they are genuine and do care for you. But they have been blinded and are being used; they are victims to the tricks and schemes of the enemy, just as you. These are people that will say, "I don't know what came over me..." They have not built themselves up in their innermost being, so the flesh is weak.

I am talking from experience; I must have been used many times by the devil unknowingly. I was blinded and did not know the truth of God. I have also had my fair share of abuse and misuse in friendships and in family; I had to learn over time the source of what I was dealing with. The real source of what we're dealing with is a real enemy that is set out for you so that you may never know who you truly are. So that you may never experience the plans and purpose that God has for your life.
This is true; we learn by experience.

Now I thank God for His faithfulness in delivering me from my own mind, because had He not, I would have been keeping the wrong enemies close, thinking that I should "keep my enemy close." I would have been keeping every curse that is attached to this word, "enemy." This is what we are made to believe; we are made to believe that our friends/family that have hurt us are the enemy.

Let me first tell you what the enemy is not....

▫️The enemy is not your friend who stole from you.
▫️The enemy is not your boyfriend/husband, wife girlfriend, sister, brother, mother, or father
▫️The enemy is not the job that you lost.
▫️The enemy is not the person that you lost and had to bury.
▫️The enemy is not that person that manipulated you.
▫️The enemy is not the person or child that lied to you.
▫️The enemy is not the man that raped you.
▫️The enemy is not the person that deceived you.
▫️The enemy is not your teacher, manager, or pastor that has said NO.

The biggest enemy is what we choose to believe way after the incident has occurred. What you allow in your mind and what you choose to believe as truth. Your mind is your greatest enemy. "Guard your heart, for out of it flows the issues of life." What this scripture is saying is that we must guard our mind. The heart is the mind; the mind captures all events and decides. It triggers the heart in the direction to follow.

The enemy is the unforgiveness that we have chosen to keep. The enemy is the spirit behind the vessel. Yes, the person in their ignorance and weakness may have allowed that spirit to enter that was used against you, but ultimately you have the choice to decide what you allow in your heart; this is especially when you are born again because you understand truth. The vessel that was used was weak because they are not born again; they don't know right from wrong.

Now as a believer, what you allow will be allowed, and what you disallow will be disallowed. Now I know that it hurts, that it pains when you have been taken for granted or taken advantage of, and in a moment you are allowed to be upset and angry, but you can't stay there. For your own well-being, you cannot choose to be swallowed up.

God has given us freedom of choice. Freedom to choose: choose this day what you will choose. Will it be bitterness, hatred, anger, rejection, abuse... Now these are the real enemies that you have chosen to keep close. These are the enemies when you choose not to forgive.

Now let's talk about every other enemy that stems from what I have mentioned above.

Unforgiveness leads to sickness, poverty, stagnation, and all sorts of fears. You lose the joy that God gives you from companionship because of lack of trust. Not being able to trust others can cripple you. The Bible says, "We wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against rulers of darkness, and against wickedness in high places" (Ephesians 6:12).

Flesh and blood are people, vessels that can either carry the presence of God in them or carry the kingdom of darkness.

What does the word of God say? We should not wrestle with people. Why? How will you win a person? how will you become a witness How is a person supposed to see the difference between light and evil if you are standing for light and fighting back by using the tactics of darkness... "keeping your enemy close"? To do what, though?

How can the world be saved if the righteous is scarcely saved? 1st Peter 4:18 So now when you hear people say, Keep your friends close but your enemies closer... now you know that the enemy you are keeping closer will not benefit you in any way. If you still decide to keep the person you believe is the enemy close to you for whatever reasons, then I have breaking news for you... You will fight a losing battle; like I mentioned earlier, our war is not with flesh. The real war is with the one you can't see. The reason you can't see it is because you don't have spiritual insight in the Kingdom. Jesus has already defeated hell, death, and the grave, and through faith in Him, we become partakers of all that He has. We are collaborators and partakers in His kingdom. Jesus is the only way, the truth, and the light, and no man can come to the Father except through Him, by believing in Him.

So if you keep your enemies close, I urge you this day to be loosed from the bondage that the enemy has trapped you in; be free in your mind and repent from this transgression. Now whom the Son sets free is free indeed. Start believing in the one who is able to deliver you from every affliction, Jesus Christ.
